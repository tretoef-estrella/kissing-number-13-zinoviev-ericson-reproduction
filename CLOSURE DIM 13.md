### §13.12.1 Motivation and scope

V12's closure (v5 §13) established that the native integer shell of ZE99
($\|v\|^2 = 16$ over $\{-4,-2,-1,0,1,2,4\}^{13}$ with $\sqrt{3}$
extension for the F orbits) admits no multi-swap path to 1155 with
$k \leq 5$. The 1,608-candidate cset $\leq 4$ catalog was built and
exhaustively searched; F-A-bis-1 through F-A-bis-4 were registered as
paper-grade.

The Architect, on 07 May 2026 immediately following the V12 closure,
raised an explicit objection: **all V11+V12 attacks targeted delta = +1
(target size 1155), but no attack at delta = +2, +3, +4 had been
executed.** The objection was anchored mathematically by the parallel
with the Architect's own combinatorial design search on Diamante
22-6-13, where the empirical observation "excess = 2 and excess = 5
never appeared" while excess values 0, 3, 4, 6, ... did, demonstrates that
some target values are structurally forbidden by parity. The hypothesis
extended naturally: if the cset distribution against ZE99 admits parity
$\text{cset} \in \{0, 2, 4, 6, ...\}$ (empirically confirmed across
two shells: F-frente-A-2 in $\mathbb{Q} + \mathbb{Q}\sqrt{3}$ norm² = 24
and F-A-bis-2 in native norm² = 16), perhaps the **target-size
distribution** also admits parity, with even deltas 1156 / 1158 reachable
where odd delta 1155 is forbidden.

The objection was registered as **Grieta G** (size-parity hypothesis,
v5 §1.7) and scheduled for immediate sandbox attack as Phase 4 of Frente
A-bis. The cost was negligible: ~5 minutes wall-clock on the existing
catalog with relaxed pruning, no engine compilation, no new catalog
generation.

### §13.12.2 Methodology

The Phase 4 search reuses the V12 catalog (1,608 candidates, cset $\leq 4$,
norm² = 16, integer alphabet with $\sqrt{3}$ extension) without
modification. The pairwise compatibility matrix and the cset-set per
candidate are preserved.

The search criterion changes: for target delta = $d$ and clique size
$k$, the relaxed pruning is

$$|U(S)| \leq k - d \quad \text{where } U(S) = \bigcup_{v \in S} C(v)$$

instead of the V12 criterion $|U| \leq k - 1$ (which was the delta = +1
case). A k-clique $S$ satisfying $|U(S)| \leq k - d$ produces a
configuration of size $1154 - |U| + k \geq 1154 - (k - d) + k = 1154 + d$.

The sweep is parameterized over $d \in \{+2, +3, +4\}$ and
$k \in [\max(2, d+1), 7]$. The upper bound $k = 7$ is chosen by
budget-driven calculation: the visit count at $|U| \leq 5$ is on the
order of $10^6$, manageable on Mac M2 25% CPU in ~5 minutes; at
$|U| \leq 6$ the visit count would jump to ~$10^7$, requiring ~50
minutes and yielding diminishing returns under the F-A-bis-3 + F-A-bis-4
mechanism.

Backtracking is identical to V12 Phase 3: lexicographic ordering of
candidates (cset = 2 first, then cset = 4), per-vertex starting expansion,
U-budget pruning at every depth.

**Verification at runtime**: each k-clique candidate satisfying the
$|U|$-budget inequality is verified by recomputing all $\binom{k}{2}$
pairwise inner products in exact integer arithmetic over
$\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z}\sqrt{3}$ and confirming all are $\leq 8$.
